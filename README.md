This documents the rebuild of an Americas partner program for a CRM/BPM platform moving from vendor-led implementation toward partner-led distribution and delivery.

The program began with 30 partners, inconsistent qualification, and limited channel-generated pipeline. The company had made a deliberate choice: invest in R&D to advance the platform, and build a partner network to own delivery and fully cover the Americas. The program needed an extended salesforce, implementation partners to own delivery, and technology partners bringing outside tools, integration connectors for common software, and BPM process templates to the marketplace.

The sections below document the decisions, operating mechanisms, and results of that rebuild. While the examples are specific to this program, the concepts show how the same work can be approached for any program.

## Result

- 30 partners inherited
- 18 removed through audit
- 12 partners retained on new terms
- 45 net-new partners recruited
- 57 partners on the final roster
- 60% SIs, 30% VARs, 5% Reference Partners, 5% Technology Partners among the 45 net-new partners
- Delivery reach from Alaska to Chile
- Partners closing first independent implementations within the first quarter of certification
- 13 of the 45 recruited partners still active today, nearly a decade past my tenure
